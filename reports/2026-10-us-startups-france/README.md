## Method

Five Opus research agents swept TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, Axios Pro Rata, Fortune Term Sheet, FinSMEs, AlleyWatch, sector trade press, press-release wires and accelerator announcements. Four Opus analyst agents then scored each company against one rubric (market demand 25, regulatory fit 15, strategic fit 20, readiness 25, R&D and talent 15). The supervisor merged, de-duplicated, re-checked 33 companies' round, date and headquarters by independent search, harmonised the readiness component with one formula (capital base by round size plus a European-signal bonus assigned from verified evidence), and applied two documented overrides (Hertha Metals, Ascerta).

## Verification limits

The research environment could not open publisher pages (network policy), so facts rest on two or more independent search results and, for part of the set, full article text from the Bigdata.com news index. Confidence "medium" marks entries where one fact (usually founding year, exact date or city) rests on a single source. Search budgets ran out before Techstars, a16z speedrun, Plug and Play and the full Y Combinator Fall 2026 cohorts were covered; HR tech, edtech, mobility and creator-economy rounds are thin. Two New York entries (AIR Security, Chamelio) have most of their team in Israel. Removed after discovery: Ridge Biotechnologies (Founded 2020 (BusinessWire/Crunchbase); outside the 2021+ rule). France notes, pitches and scores are analyst assessments, not company statements.
