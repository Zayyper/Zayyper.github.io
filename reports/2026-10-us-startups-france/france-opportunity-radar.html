How the scoring works

Each company was scored by a sector analyst against one rubric, then reviewed by a supervisor for consistency. The five components add to 100. Tier A is 70 and above, B is 50 to 69, C is 30 to 49, D is below 30.

ComponentMaxWhat it measures
Market demand25Addressable French or EU customer base for this exact product within 24 months; US-only business models score low.
Regulatory fit15Whether EU and French rules are a tailwind (AI Act, NIS2, DORA, CSRD, AGEC, nuclear revival, defence spending) or a headwind (licensing walls, ITAR, US reimbursement).
Strategic fit20Named French corporates, labs and clusters that are natural customers or partners, and alignment with France 2030 priorities.
Readiness25Capital, stage and international intent: round size, named EU customers or offices, European investors, founders' EU ties.
R&D and talent15The case for a French engineering, research, clinical or industrial site: CIR research tax credit, JEI status, CEA, INRIA, Inserm, clinical networks, decarbonised power.

Opportunity types. Market entry (sell in France), R&D hub, industrial site (plant, pilot or test site), clinical (trials and regulatory path through French centres), corporate partnership, VC investment (French or EU investors in the next round), talent recruiting.

Sourcing and verification

Discovery. Five researchers swept TechCrunch, Crunchbase News, Axios Pro Rata, Fortune Term Sheet, FinSMEs, AlleyWatch, trade press (Endpoints, Fierce Biotech, Canary Media, SpaceNews), press-release wires and accelerator announcements for the window 1 September to 6 October 2026. Inclusion rules: US headquarters with a verifiable city and state; pre-seed to Series B, stealth exit, launch or accelerator cohort inside the window; founded 2021 or later.

Verification limits. The research environment could not open publisher pages directly, so facts were checked against two or more independent search results and, for part of the set, full article text from the Bigdata.com news index. The supervisor independently re-checked 33 companies' round, date and headquarters by search and found no contradictions. "Confidence: medium" marks entries where one fact (usually founding year, exact date or city) rests on a single source. Each fiche lists the public URLs used.

Known gaps. Search budgets ran out before Techstars, a16z speedrun, Plug and Play and the full Y Combinator Fall 2026 cohort lists were worked through; HR tech, edtech, mobility and creator-economy rounds are thin. 1 company was removed by the supervisor after discovery: Ridge Biotechnologies (Founded 2020 (BusinessWire/Crunchbase); outside the 2021+ rule). Two New York entries (AIR Security, Chamelio) have most of their team in Israel and are flagged on their fiches.

France notes, pitches and scores are the analysts' assessment of fit, not statements by the companies. Amounts are in US dollars as disclosed; "disclosed capital" sums the event rounds only.
