## Ema at a glance

Ema sells "AI employees": pre-built teams of AI agents that run HR, IT and finance work in large enterprises. It has raised $140M, reports bookings above $150M and closed a $77M Series B on 23 September 2026 to expand into EMEA and APAC [1][2]. For France, its key asset is an exclusive global go-to-market deal with Teleperformance (TP), signed in April 2025 [17].

## Product and business model

Ema sells configurable agents that complete multi-step back-office tasks, priced on outcomes rather than seats. A Generative Workflow Engine builds workflows; the EmaFusion layer mixes outputs from 100+ models per task (company claim) [21].

- **Packaging.** An HR, IT and Finance Hub of pre-trained AI employees launched on 1 September 2026. It covers onboarding, benefits, headcount planning, access, identity and ticketing [13].
- **Integration.** 250+ connectors, including Workday and ServiceNow [13][24]. Agents run in Teams, Slack, Google Chat and voice [15][31].
- **Languages.** 30+, French included (company) [22].
- **Buyers.** Shared-service centres of large firms, and IT-services resellers [1][17].
- **Pricing.** No public price list. Ema charges for completed tasks and outcomes, not seats or tokens [4][25]. Contracts follow a guided pilot (single source) [27].
- **Deployment.** On-premises or air-gapped inside the client's own Azure or Google Cloud tenancy [23][31].
- **Certifications.** SOC 2 Type I and II, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 42001, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST AI RMF, NIST CSF and NIST SP 800-171, per the company [19][20]. A June 2025 company blog claims "full alignment with the EU AI Act"; this is self-declared [19].
- **Hosting.** No EU data region is named in any source found. One listing mentions "data residency capabilities" (single source) [31].

## Regulatory fit for France and the EU

Certifications are a tailwind; HR use cases, French works-council law and US hosting are headwinds.

- **EU AI Act.** AI used for hiring, promotion, task allocation or performance monitoring is high-risk [43]. The Digital Omnibus delays these duties to 2 December 2027 for standalone systems [44]. Its publication on 24 July 2026 is reported (single source) [44]. HR helpdesk agents may fall outside high-risk scope; coaching and headcount planning need review.
- **GDPR and CNIL.** HR data needs a processing agreement, a DPIA and a position on US transfers.
- **Works councils.** French courts require CSE consultation before AI tools are deployed, even as pilots: Nanterre, 14 February 2025; Créteil, 15 July 2025; Paris, 2 September 2025 [45].
- **DORA and NIS2.** Financial and NIS2-covered clients will impose third-party security clauses.
- **What Ema needs.** An EU data region, French DPA and DPIA templates, a CSE briefing pack, and an AI Act classification per agent.
