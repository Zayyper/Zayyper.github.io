## Hang Ten Systems at a glance

Hang Ten Systems sells small AI-assisted engineering teams as an alternative to systems integrators for enterprise software work [1][5]. Former Infosys CEO Vishal Sikka founded it in May 2026; it raised $85M in two seed rounds announced 12 weeks apart [4][6]. Two of its three named customers are European, and it targets the work French ESNs sell today.

## Product and business model

Hang Ten sells software outcomes delivered by small expert teams whose AI agents write most of the code. The company calls this an "AI-native delivery model" with three parts [3][8]:

- **Agentic code generation** to build, change and run enterprise software "at a fraction of the cost and time" (company claim) [3].
- **A reusable skills library.** One outlet names the in-house framework "Hobie", which packages AI skills for regulated industries (single source) [16].
- **An "FDE Bench"** of forward-deployed engineers placed inside client environments [3][13].

Services cover advisory, transformation and applied AI [5]. Domains are enterprise transformations, finance, HR, product development and migrations [3][6]. Rajagopalan said teams of two to four people handle some projects that once needed about 30 [1]. Customers or third parties still do final quality checks and certification [1]. Sikka said the build part "has basically become close to zero marginal cost, close to zero time" [2]. Mayfield's thesis: services scale linearly with headcount, while Hang Ten's leverage "grows with every project" [7].

- **Pricing:** not disclosed. Press cites "multi-million-dollar contracts across advisory and development engagements" [6].
- **AI models used:** not disclosed.
- **Vianai's contribution:** no technology transfer is documented. Sikka left Vianai in April 2026, saying it was "going through a transaction" (single source) [2]. The CTO and design chief came from Vianai [26][27]. Vianai's hila analytics agent has no documented link [28].
- **Certifications and hosting:** none found (no SOC 2, ISO 27001 or EU hosting statement).

## Team

The team is Sikka plus long-time colleagues from SAP, Infosys and Vianai. Sikka spent 12 years at SAP, was its first CTO, led HANA and joined the executive board in 2010 [21]. In 2014 he became Infosys's first non-founder CEO [21]. He resigned in August 2017, citing a "continuous drumbeat of distractions" amid a dispute with founders [22]. He founded Vianai in 2019 with $50M [21]. He sits on Oracle's board [23]. Stanford HAI lists a BMW supervisory board seat; GSK lists him as a director; current status unverified [24][25].

## Regulatory fit for France and the EU

French rules are a moderate headwind for a small US firm with Saudi and Singaporean backers. They also create compliance work Hang Ten could sell.

- **GDPR:** agents will touch HR and finance data. Processor contracts and transfer safeguards for non-EU staff are needed.
- **EU AI Act:** the Digital Omnibus, adopted 29 June 2026, moved Annex III high-risk duties to 2 Dec 2027 [43]. Transparency and general-purpose AI duties kept 2 Aug 2026 [43]. HR systems built for clients may count as high-risk.
- **NIS2:** France's "résilience" bill, supervised by ANSSI, reaches the National Assembly from 7 Oct 2026 [44][45]. Energy and health clients will impose supply-chain security clauses.
- **SecNumCloud:** a 2026 decree made it enforceable for certain sensitive State data [46][47].
- **Works councils:** on 14 Feb 2025 a Nanterre court suspended an AI deployment pending CSE consultation [48]. Contracts must be deliverable-based to avoid illicit labour-lending rules.
- **Certifications:** none documented; ISO 27001 is a minimum step.

## Risks and open questions

- Delivery claims are self-reported [14].
- About 25 staff may fail large-account supplier referencing [5].
- No EU entity, hosting, certification or French-language capacity.
- US ownership, state-linked Saudi and Singaporean investors and agent access to client systems raise sovereignty questions.
- Aramco is investor and customer.
- Sikka's Infosys exit followed a governance dispute [22].
- Incumbents copy the model: Capgemini, Infosys with Devin [36][38].
- Open: AI models used, data location, pricing, the Vianai transaction, valuation, round labels [5][19].
