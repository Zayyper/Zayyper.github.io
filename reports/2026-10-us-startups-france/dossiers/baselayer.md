## Baselayer at a glance

Baselayer sells business identity, credit and fraud-risk data to US banks, fintechs, lenders and government agencies, and now applies it to AI agents. It says 2,300+ financial institutions and payments firms use its network, and it announced a $35M Series A led by M13 on 22 September 2026 [1][2][6]. For France, it is a US-built KYB graph arriving as the EU anti-money-laundering rulebook (AMLR) applies from 10 July 2027 [30].

## Product and business model

Baselayer sells API and dashboard access to a business identity graph for onboarding, underwriting and monitoring. Modules cover business verification, a KYB rating, lien, lawsuit and bankruptcy search, website and social analysis, risk rating and portfolio monitoring [10]. Monitoring tracks Secretary of State filings [20].

- **Data.** The company says it fuses public records, IRS data, sanctions lists, web signals and fraud telemetry from 2,200+ institutions [18]. It claims a 98% match rate (single source) [18].
- **Agentic Identity Suite (September 2026).** "Know Your Agent" checks who deployed an AI agent, whom it represents and whether it may transact, using cryptographic verification [1][7]. An "agentic fraud consortium" was also announced [2].
- **Buyers.** Banks, credit unions, lenders, payments firms and government agencies [9][11].
- **Pricing.** Not disclosed. The only documented model is pay-per-request access bought by agents through Nevermined's x402 card delegation [25][26].
- **Differentiation.** One comparison rates Middesk stronger on US data breadth and Baselayer on risk rules for high-risk sectors (single source) [28].
- **International data.** International Business Search is API-only and uses a third-party provider [27]. One docs version lists 33 countries; another cites 76 jurisdictions including France, Germany and the UK [27]. A registrations search route was added on 13 July 2026 [29].
- **Certifications and hosting.** No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or hosting region found.

## Regulatory fit for France and the EU

EU rules raise demand for KYB data but place duties on a US vendor.

- **AMLR and AMLA (tailwind).** AMLR applies from 10 July 2027 as one rulebook enforced by the new AML Authority [30]. Beneficial ownership is tested by ownership and control at 25% [30]. Register discrepancies must be reported within 14 days (single source) [30].
- **AMLD6 and the French UBO register (headwind).** Since 31 July 2024, INPI limits register access to a legitimate interest; obliged professionals keep access [36]. This follows the CJEU ruling of 22 November 2022 [36]. Baselayer would rely on its clients' access.
- **French registry data (tailwind).** Since 1 January 2023, the RNE national register is free on data.inpi.fr [37]. APIs update company data daily and include annual accounts since 2017 [38].
- **GDPR (headwind).** Directors and owners are personal data. Pooling fraud signals across French banks needs a legal basis, an impact assessment and transfer safeguards.
- **EU AI Act (watch).** Credit scoring of natural persons is high-risk; fraud detection is carved out [39]. The Digital Omnibus moved high-risk duties to 2 December 2027 (single source) [40]. Scores on sole traders could fall in scope (analyst view).
- **DORA (neutral).** Since 17 January 2025, banks register each ICT third-party contract [45]. Baselayer would face audit and exit clauses.
- **PSD3 and PSR.** Not verified in this pass.

Baselayer would need EU hosting, an EU GDPR representative, audit reports and French registry integration.

## Risks and open questions

- The graph is US-only; EU coverage rests on one third-party provider, and the docs disagree on 33 versus 76 jurisdictions.
- Funding history is unclear. Is the August $20M part of the $35M? The October 2024 "$20M seed" lists Brazilian funds and may be a data error.
- The founder roster is unclear (Slessman).
- No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or EU hosting evidence.
- GDPR may limit cross-bank fraud pooling, the core of the model.
- The shift to AI-agent identity may push EU KYB down the roadmap.
- Sumsub, Kyckr, Ondorse and Pappers already hold French registry access.
