## Ascerta France launch plan at a glance

**Verdict: conditional go, as a cheap option and not a business.** The condition is a signed, non-exclusive referral agreement by 31 March 2027. It must pay at least 10% of first-year contract value and register leads for 12 months. Until then, the operator spends at most €1,000 and 5 h/week.

**Thesis.** Ascerta has the strongest French hook of the small US AI-cost vendors: Atos uses it in its Sovereign Agentic Studios [1][2]. But it is small: 9 staff at seed in May 2025, current headcount not disclosed [3]. Its Series A release has no Europe plan [1]. Searches on 7 October 2026 found no partner programme, no EMEA hiring and no news since 6 October [4]. It has no stated certification or EU hosting [5]. It will not pay a French rep in 2027. The only realistic deal is a success-fee referral, so the operator pays for Ascerta's French market test. It is worth doing only because the loss is capped: about €2,500 and 125 hours.

## What you would sell and to whom

**Product.** Ascerta, formerly Pay-i, measures enterprise AI spend below the token level, by person, team and tool, and ties it to business outcomes. It has three modules: Atlas (ROI), Forge (coding agents) and Convoy (self-provisioned capacity). It connects to Azure OpenAI, Foundry, Copilot, Bedrock AgentCore, Agentforce, Databricks and Claude Code [5]. The pitch: "Can you prove what your Copilot and agent bill returns?"

**Buyer persona.** A DSI or Chief Data & AI Officer at a large group with a big Copilot or Azure OpenAI rollout; the champion is a FinOps lead. Blockers: the DPO (per-person tracking), the RSSI (no certification), the CSE, which must be consulted on staff-monitoring tools (Code du travail L2312-38) [6], and procurement, including DORA at banks and insurers [5].

**Targets** (from the dossier [5]):
1. **Atos.** Already a user through the Scaler cohort [2]. The aim is resale inside SAS deals. Weakness: a €504M H1 2026 net loss [7].
2. **Capgemini.** Its RAISE asset promises LLMs "at a controlled cost" [8]. A channel, not a buyer, and possibly a rival.
3. **Sopra Steria.** Holds a Caisse des Dépôts framework worth up to €140M for generative AI [9].
4. **AXA.** Runs Secure GPT for 140,000 staff and is rolling out Copilot [10]. Fits the $3M insurer case. DORA applies.
5. **BNP Paribas.** Runs an internal LLM-as-a-Service on shared GPUs to cut costs [11]. Fits Convoy.

All five are CAC40-scale. In year 1, Atos is the best route.

**Price band.** Not published [12]. Benchmarks: CloudZero's median contract is $57k to $78k a year [13]. Langfuse Enterprise cloud costs $2,499 a month [14]. Portkey's enterprise tiers run $2k to $10k+ a month [15]. *Estimate* for Ascerta: a paid pilot of €15k to €30k, and a first annual contract of €40k to €100k. The reasoning: it sells above the gateway tools and near the FinOps suites.

**Sales cycle.** Benchmarks: 115 days for contracts of $20k to $60k a year, and 180 days above $60k [16]. *Estimate* for France: 6 to 12 months to a paid order, and 3 to 5 months to a paid pilot. The DPO, CSE, security and procurement steps add 2 to 4 months.

## The deal with Ascerta

**Options, most realistic first:**
- **Referral partner (apporteur d'affaires).** The operator earns a success fee only, so Ascerta pays nothing up front. The norm is 10% to 20% of first-year contract value, usually for year 1 only [17][18]. **This is the target.**
- **Agent commercial (Code de commerce L134-1 and following) [19].** This protects the operator best. The termination indemnity cannot be waived, and courts usually award two years of gross commissions [20]. An agent working in the EU keeps it even under US law (CJEU *Ingmar*) [21]. Negotiating without pricing power can still count as agency (CJEU *Trendsetteuse*) [22]. RSAC registration costs €23.86 in 2026 [23]. Ask for it, but expect a refusal because of the indemnity.
- **Reseller.** Margins run 15% to 30% on new business and 5% to 15% on renewals [18]. But the operator would carry credit risk and support, and CAC40 procurement will not list a one-person reseller. **No.**
- **Contracted France lead on a retainer.** This needs a budget of about €3k to €6k a month (estimate). Under 5% in 2027.

**What to ask for:**
1. **Commission:** 15% of first-year value, paid on cash collected (floor 10%), plus 5% on year-2 renewals.
2. **Territory:** France, non-exclusive.
3. **Lead registration:** 12-month protection per registered account, **including deals closed through Atos or Wipro**.
4. **Pilot pricing:** authority to quote within an approved band (€15k to €30k, 90 days).
5. **Security pack within 30 days:** certification status, hosting regions, DPA, sub-processors and DORA clauses.
6. **Support:** a demo tenant, a named Ascerta sales engineer and a monthly pipeline review.
7. **Path to a paid role:** a France lead role with a retainer if sourced annual revenue reaches €150k by 31 Dec 2027.

**Ascerta's likely answer (estimate).** No reply: 40%. "Send leads, we'll look after you", with nothing signed: 30%. A formal referral at 10% for year 1 with 90-day registration: 25%. A retainer: 5% or less. The reasoning: after a Series A the priority is US enterprise, and Wipro and Atos already cover European delivery [1][24].

**Fallback.** If the answer is informal, require a signed two-page referral letter before any introduction. If there is no reply, try a warm path through BGV's Paris office [25]. If the answer is no, take the pipeline to the other plans.

## Why this can fail

- **Too small to pay a rep.** The operator works unpaid for 6 to 12 months to test Ascerta's market for it.
- **Silence.** After a Series A, the focus is the US. There is no Europe plan or partner programme [1][4].
- **Not ready for the EU.** No certification, EU hosting or DPA has been found [5]. At AXA and BNP, DORA and security reviews can stop a deal.
- **Staff tracking.** Measuring AI use per person triggers GDPR duties and CSE consultation [6], which adds months.
- **Channel conflict.** Deals may route through Atos or Wipro [2][24]. Without lead registration, the operator's leads get absorbed.
- **Free substitutes.** Microsoft Foundry, OpenAI's FinOps features, Langfuse and Capgemini RAISE are often "good enough" [5][8].
- **A weak reference.** Atos is losing money [7], and its paid-production status is unknown [5].
- **Calendar.** 115 to 180-day cycles plus French procurement push the first commission into 2028 [16].
- **Thin capacity.** 7 h/week means about one serious meeting a week, against CAC40 buying committees.
