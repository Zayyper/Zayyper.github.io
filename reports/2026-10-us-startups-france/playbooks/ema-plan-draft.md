## Ema France launch plan at a glance

**Verdict: conditional go, as a cheap 16-week option and not as a business.** The condition: by Friday 29 January 2027 the operator holds a written Ema referral agreement covering France. It must set (a) at least 10% of first-year contract value, pilots included; (b) deal registration with 12 months' protection; (c) a written rule on which accounts belong to Teleperformance (TP). If not, the operator stops and applies for an Ema EMEA sales or partner role, or a seat in TP's AI practice: employment is the realistic way to earn real money from Ema in 2027.

**Thesis.** The TP exclusivity makes an independent operator marginal, not pointless. TP is Ema's "exclusive global go-to-market partner and integrator" "for 400+ clients" [1][2]. Yet Ema lists other partners (PwC, KPMG, Microsoft, Google, NTT Data, Wipro), runs reseller and affiliate programmes [3][4] and hires its own UK sellers and a UK Partner Manager [5][6]. So the exclusivity appears to cover TP's clients, not all of Europe (terms are private). Time, not TP, kills the economics. Ema has no French entity, staff, customer or documented EU data region. French HR deployments need works-council (CSE) consultation, even for pilots [21][22]. Enterprise cycles run past 31 December 2027. The likeliest 2027 cash outcome is zero; the value is a 2028 option and a route to an Ema role.

## What you would sell and to whom

**Product.** Ema sells "AI employees": pre-built agents that resolve HR, IT and finance requests end to end (onboarding, benefits, access, ticket triage). They run on Workday, ServiceNow and 250+ other connectors, inside Teams or Slack [7][8]. Pricing is per AI employee, workflow or action, after a guided pilot; there is no public price list [9][10]. Reference case: a Wipro HR assistant serving 240,000 staff in 65+ countries [11].

**Buyer persona.** The head of HR shared services, reporting to the group HR director (DRH); co-buyer, the IT service-management lead. Blockers: the data-protection officer (DPO), security chief (RSSI), purchasing, the CSE.

**Five target accounts.** Fit hypotheses. Whether each is a TP client is unknown and must be cleared with Ema first.
1. **Capgemini.** The French analogue of the Wipro case, with a huge internal HR helpdesk. Risk: it builds its own agents, for example with NVIDIA [28].
2. **Sodexo.** A frontline-heavy, many-country workforce with high HR query volume.
3. **Orange.** Group HR and IT service desks; a strong union and CSE culture means slow consultation.
4. **Schneider Electric.** A digitally mature global buyer of shared services, exposed to ServiceNow bundling.
5. **Engie.** Centralised support functions; critical-infrastructure security reviews will slow any deal.

Sopra Steria is a rival: with Computacenter it won a sovereign AI framework worth up to €140M at Caisse des Dépôts [29].

**Price band (estimate).** Paid pilot €40k–80k; first-year contract €100k–250k. Anchor: Moveworks, the closest public benchmark, has a median of about $130k a year, $100k–200k for 1,000–5,000 employees, plus $50k–200k of implementation [12].

**Sales cycle (estimate).** Four to seven months to a paid pilot; 9–15 months to production. That is 8–16 weeks of implementation [12], plus 1–3 months of security and GDPR review, plus 1–3 months of CSE consultation [21][22], plus supplier onboarding.

## The deal with Ema and TP

**Realistic relationship: a non-exclusive referral partner (apporteur d'affaires) to Ema, not an agent commercial.** Under Code de commerce L134-1, an agent commercial negotiates for a principal on a permanent basis and registers on the RSAC (the commercial-agents register, about €25 [14]). At the end of the contract the agent is owed an indemnity, which courts usually set at two years of gross commissions; the claim must be notified within a year (L134-12) [13]. Under the EU court's *Ingmar* ruling, choosing Californian law does not remove it [15], so Ema will refuse the status. Commission norms: 2–15% for French referrers [16], 5–20% in B2B SaaS [17]; for SaaS agents, an estimated 10–20%. Reselling is out: Ema resellers handle licensing, first-line support and onboarding [3].

| Ask | Likely answer (estimate) |
|---|---|
| Ema: 10–15% of first-year value, pilots included, paid within 30 days of the customer paying | "Join the affiliate programme"; its rates are not public [4] |
| Ema: deal registration, 12 months' protection, a written TP rule | Registration yes; TP's list no; conflicts go TP's way |
| Ema: after two accepted opportunities, a six-month "France lead" retainer of €3k–5k a month (estimate) | "Show pipeline first"; only if a France hire is planned |
| Ema: demo access, French compliance documents, a named UK partner contact | Partly; the UK Partner Manager role is open [5] |
| TP: TP named as integrator on every deal the operator brings | Interest, no contract; onboarding a sole trader as a supplier is slow |

**Fallback.** With no agreement by 29 January 2027, the operator applies to Ema's EMEA roles [5][6] with the account map and compliance pack, and approaches TP's AI practice (TP.ai FAB) [30].

## Why this can fail

- **TP decides conflicts.** The exclusivity terms and TP's client list are private, and TP may invest up to $15M in Ema [1]. Contested accounts go to TP.
- **Ema is not "French-ready."** No French entity, staff, customer or documented EU data region; DPOs and RSSIs will stall.
- **CSE and regulation add months.** French courts required CSE consultation before AI deployments, pilots included (Nanterre, February 2025; later Créteil and Paris) [21][22]. The EU AI Act's high-risk employment duties apply from 2 December 2027 [31], so buyers may wait.
- **Incumbents bundle.** ServiceNow closed its $2.85B purchase of Moveworks [32]. Microsoft and Workday sell agents inside their accounts. French buyers favour sovereign AI: Sopra Steria [29], Mistral, Dust.
- **Credibility gap.** An unknown part-timer who cannot run demos will struggle to reach group HR directors.
- **No protection, bad timing.** A referral partner has no statutory indemnity; Ema can change terms, and a 2027 France hire could absorb the pipeline. Even a perfect year pays in 2028.
- **TP distraction.** TP guides 0–2% growth for 2026 [26] and expects €120M–140M of restructuring costs [27].

## Kill criteria

- **Fri 6 Nov 2026.** No reply from any named Ema contact after three touches: stop working on Ema; try TP once.
- **Fri 29 Jan 2027.** No agreement meeting the verdict condition: stop the independent route and apply for roles.
- **Fri 30 Apr 2027.** Fewer than 3 accepted opportunities, or no Ema seller at a discovery meeting: stop.
- **Fri 16 Jul 2027.** No written pilot proposal: holding mode at 1 h a week or less, no more cash.
- **Fri 17 Dec 2027.** No pilot or purchase order: no 2028 spending unless a deal is in purchasing with a CSE date set.
- **Any date.** Ema refuses commission on a registered deal, or spending passes €4,030: stop.
