# France Launch Playbooks (working files)

The reviewed, corrected report is the Claude Doc "France Launch Playbooks" (7 October 2026). These files are the inputs it was built from:

- `ascerta-plan-draft.md`, `ema-plan-draft.md`, `air-security-plan-draft.md`: the three analysts' first drafts (before review; numbers in these drafts were later corrected).
- `review.md`: the independent reviewer's findings (23 items: 1 blocker, 9 major, 13 minor) and the corrections the final report applies.

Corrections applied in the final report: one gate on 26 February 2027 for all three plans; at most two plans continue; introductions-only role (no negotiating, to avoid commercial-agent reclassification); 10% commission everywhere; retainers removed from revenue; agreement odds cut to 15% / 25% / 15%; works-council consultation of 1 to 3 months before any pilot; VAT added for a micro-entreprise under the franchise; first cash collected not before Q1 2028 in any plan.
