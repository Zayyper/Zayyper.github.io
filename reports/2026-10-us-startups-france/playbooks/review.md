# Review of the three France launch plans (7 Oct 2026)

## Verdict per plan

- **Ascerta: accept with fixes.** Sound gates, but a CAC40 pilot by 23 Jul 2027 is not achievable, and the 15% commission and 30% odds contradict the plan's own "likely answer".
- **Ema: accept with fixes.** The most honest base case (€0), but it falls after its own kill date, 76% of weighted revenue is a retainer, and it misses the Atos route.
- **AIR: accept with fixes.** The most optimistic plan (a €60k order in 2027 at 12.5%), with no CSE step and gate odds that ignore its own likely answer.
- **All three in parallel:** not as written; see finding 1.

## Findings

1. **Blocker, all.** *Hours do not fit when the plans run in parallel.* Combined load peaks at 25.5 h/week in April 2027 (see Combined view). **Fix:** start Ascerta outbound only if Ema or AIR dies at its 29 Jan gate, or cap Ascerta at 4, Ema 7, AIR 8 and shared admin 1 h/week.
2. **Major, all.** *"Referral partner, not agent".* Under *Trendsetteuse* (C-828/18), negotiating makes someone an agent even without power over prices, and the Cour de cassation has followed it [4]. Ascerta's ask 4 (authority to quote) and AIR's retainer for "10 qualified meetings" invite the non-waivable L134-12 indemnity [1][2]. **Fix:** an introduction-only role in writing; drop ask 4; have the lawyer check the reclassification risk.
3. **Major, all.** *VAT is ignored.* A micro-entreprise under the VAT franchise (services threshold €37,500 in 2026) cannot deduct VAT [37], and the Sales Navigator price (€1,089/yr) excludes VAT [32]. **Fix:** add 20% to the tool, legal, web, data and event-pass lines: about +€1,200 combined.
4. **Major, Ascerta and AIR.** *The base commission exceeds the plans' own expected answer.* Ascerta uses 15% and AIR 12.5%, while both expect about 10%, the market norm [31]. **Fix:** at 10%, Ascerta's base is €2,500 and upside €8,500; AIR's base is €6,000 and upside commission €16,000. Recompute every ROI.
5. **Major, all.** *Agreement odds are inconsistent with the plans' own gates.* Ascerta (30%): its 25% outcome has 90-day registration, which fails the 12-month condition; use ≤15%. AIR (30%): "in progress" on certification dates fails the gate; use ≈15%. Ema (55%) counts any affiliate terms, but the plan itself puts gate items (b) and (c) at 30% by 31 Mar; use ≤25%. **Fix:** paid-customer odds become about 3–5% each (reviewer estimate).
6. **Major, Ema.** *The base case (≈50%), "Referral terms, Feb 2027", falls after the 29 Jan kill date.* **Fix:** require gate-compliant terms by 29 Jan, or move the kill to Fri 26 Feb 2027 (+≈25 h, +≈€300).
7. **Major, Ema and AIR.** *Upside revenue counts retainers the plans expect to be refused.* Ema's €16,000 retainer is 76% of its upside and €1,600 of its €2,100 weighted revenue. AIR books 6 × €2,500 = €15,000, though it asks for 3 months (€7,500) and expects "no". **Fix:** remove retainers: Ema upside €5,000 (weighted €500), AIR upside €16,000 at 10% (weighted €930).
8. **Major, Ascerta.** *A CAC40 pilot signed by 23 Jul, under 4 months after outbound starts on 1 Apr.* The plan's own estimate is 3–5 months plus 2–4 for DPO, CSE and procurement. Per-person tracking needs a CSE consultation (L2312-38), which takes 1–3 months [26], and phases 3 and 4 overlap. **Fix:** pilot from Oct 2027 and first paid deal in Q1 2028 (except through Atos SAS), so base 2027 revenue is €0.
9. **Major, AIR.** *No CSE step, and a €60k order in the base case.* Agent discovery on staff endpoints and context screening count as monitoring of employees (L2312-38), and courts have suspended AI deployments that skipped the CSE (Nanterre 14 Feb 2025; Créteil 15 Jul 2025; Paris 2 Sep 2025 [24][25]). Sanofi's procurement takes 6–12 months. **Fix:** add 1–3 months of CSE consultation before any pilot and move the pilot gate from 15 Jul to 30 Sep. Make the base case a €15k paid pilot (commission €1,500) and move the €60k order to 2028.
10. **Major, Ema.** *Atos is missing.* Atos's Scaler cohort includes Ema as well as Pay-i (Ascerta dossier [15][16]): Ema's only documented French route besides TP. **Fix:** add Atos SAS as a lead target, check it against the TP exclusivity, and share one Atos contact with the Ascerta plan.
11. **Minor, Ema.** *"CSE consultation … pilots included".* Paris (2 Sep 2025) required consultation for a generative AI platform but not for a new version of an HR chatbot [25]: the test is the effect on working conditions. In Nanterre, a "pilot" already in staff hands counted as implementation [24]. **Fix:** reword.
12. **Minor, Ema.** *Unverified facts.* The TP wording is confirmed verbatim [20], but the UK Partner Manager listing dates from 23 Apr 2025 (decoded listing ID) [22][23]; the affiliate programme is aggregator-only, without rates [19]; and the "reseller programme" and partners PwC, KPMG and Google are not in the dossier [21]. **Fix:** mark these unverified.
13. **Minor, Ema.** *Conflicts.* Sopra Steria is a "rival" here but a "plausible target" in the dossier; the funnel has 160 contacts, Phase 2 has 120. **Fix:** reconcile.
14. **Minor, Ascerta.** *Price benchmarks.* CloudZero's "median $57k to $78k" should read: median $57,330/yr (36 purchases), range $35,000–123,125 [27]. Portkey's "$2k–10k+ a month" is unsupported: Enterprise pricing is undisclosed, and Production costs $49/month [29]. Langfuse at $2,499/month is confirmed [28].
15. **Minor, AIR.** *Weak sources.* The AWS Marketplace $50k/$80k listing could not be resolved [30], so drop it as the price anchor. The CyberArk URL (`cyberark.com/R&I.pdf`) looks malformed.
16. **Minor, Ascerta.** *Cash figures.* The minimum items sum to €2,744 (€2,844 with the bank), not €2,500, and "at most €1,000 until 31 Mar" contradicts "loss capped at €2,500". **Fix:** €1,000 to 11 Dec and about €2,000 to 31 Mar.
17. **Minor, Ascerta.** *"Weighted revenue 10% × €5k".* The €5k is undefined: use 10% × €3,750 = €375, or €250 at a 10% commission. "€9 an hour" is gross; net of cost, the base case loses €2,650.
18. **Minor, AIR.** *"About €300 if killed in January"* omits the Phase 1 lawyer (€900) and Cyber Show (€50): it is €1,250. Expected cost becomes 0.7 × 1,250 + 0.3 × 5,300 = €2,465, above the €1,730 expected revenue. Phase 2 also runs at 130 h ÷ 13 weeks = 10 h/week, above the task table's 8.75.
19. **Minor, AIR.** *RC Pro at €600 uses IT-freelancer rates;* consultant cover starts at about €15/month [9]. **Fix:** one policy, about €300/yr, for all three.
20. **Minor, Ema and AIR.** *December dates.* Both go dead from 18 Dec, and Ema closes and kills on 17 Dec 2027. **Fix:** dead from 15 Dec, gates on 10 Dec.
21. **Minor, all.** *Event dates.* Confirmed: InCyber 9–11 Mar 2027 [12][13] and TechSolutions 14–15 Oct 2026 [15]. Aggregator-only (mark unconfirmed): VivaTech 16–19 Jun 2027 [11]; Cyber Show 27–28 Jan 2027, aimed at SMBs, a weak fit for banks [14]. Unannounced: Les Assises 2027, held in Monaco [16], and Big Data & AI Paris 2027 [17]. Ema should add HR Technologies France, 20–21 Jan 2027 (aggregator) [18].
22. **Minor, all.** *Small legal and tax figures.* RSAC is €24.18 in 2026 and applies only to agents [6]. A habitual referrer pays 21.2% (BIC services); 25.6% is the BNC rate [7]. SASU set-up (≈€250) holds: notice €141 excl. VAT, RCS €35.59, beneficial-owner filing €20.34 [5]. SASU accountant: €840–4,200/yr [8]. AIR: Ingmar "applies", not "probably" [3]. The two-year indemnity is court practice and can be lower after a short contract [2].
23. **Minor, Ascerta.** *Dossier caveats dropped.* "9 staff" and the "$3M insurer" case are single-source, and AXA Secure GPT is "planned for" 140,000 staff. The AI Act delay to 2 Dec 2027 is now verified (Official Journal, 24 Jul 2026) [38].

## Combined view (all three from 12 Oct 2026)

**Cash:**
- As written: €6,364 + €4,030 + €5,324 = **€15,718, which is €718 over the cap**.
- With Ascerta's shared figure: €13,254, which still pays for RC Pro and the lawyer three times.
- Deduplicated:
  - Shared base: €5,310 (RC Pro 300, bank 100, bookkeeping 240, one tool stack for 15 months 3,250, web and data 520, lawyer template 900).
  - Increments: €5,000 (Ascerta 1,500; Ema 1,200; AIR 2,300).
  - With 10% contingency: **€11,341**. With non-recoverable VAT: **≈€12,550**.
  - This **fits €15,000 only after deduplication**.

**Hours (Ascerta + Ema + AIR, h/week):**

| Period | Load | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 2026 | 5 + 8 + 8 | **21** |
| Nov 2026 – Jan 2027 | 5 + 7.4 + 5.3 | 17.7 |
| Feb – Mar 2027 | 5 + 8 + 10 | **23** |
| Apr 2027 | 7.5 + 8 + 10 | **25.5** |
| May – Jul 2027 | 7.5 + 8 + 8 | **23.5** |
| Aug 2027 | 1 + 1.5 + 2 | 4.5 |
| Sep – Dec 2027 | 7.5 + 6 + 8 | **21.5** |

Total: 1,251 h over 63.6 weeks (19.7 h/week on average). The average fits but the peaks do not, with no time budgeted for shared admin or the shared accounts (AXA, BNP, Capgemini, Atos).

**Probability-weighted revenue by 31 Dec 2027 (the plans' own odds):**
- Ascerta: 10% × €5,000 = €500
- Ema: 0.1 × €21,000 = €2,100
- AIR: 0.30 × (0.25 × €7,500 + 0.10 × €39,000) = €1,732.5
- **Total ≈ €4,330**, against about €12,550 of cost

Without retainers: €2,283. Also at 10% commission: €333 + €500 + €930 = **≈€1,760**, little of it collected in 2027.
